Privacy policy

Spendly is a personal finance tracker. Your financial data is yours: we store it only so you can use the app, we never sell it, and you can export or delete it at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and a password. The password is stored only as a one-way scrypt hash — nobody, including us, can read it.
  • What you enter: transactions (amount, category, date, description), budgets, savings goals, recurring items and your display preferences (currency, theme).
  • Technical data: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and standard server logs (IP address, time, page requested) kept by our hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.

We do not connect to your bank, ask for card or account numbers, or use advertising or tracking cookies.

Receipt scanning

{% if receipt_ai %}

When you scan or share a receipt, the image is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) only to read the amount, date and payee. Spendly doesn't keep the image. Only the transaction you choose to save is stored.

{% else %}

When you scan or share a receipt, the image is read on your own device. Only the recognised text is sent to our server, to fill in the form. (If you share a receipt from another app before Spendly is fully installed, the image passes through our server once to reach the scan page; it is not stored.) Only the transaction you choose to save is kept.

{% endif %}

How we use it

  • To show you your dashboard, analytics, budgets and goals.
  • To send account emails you need: confirming your address and resetting your password. No marketing emails.
  • To keep the service secure, e.g. limiting repeated failed sign-in attempts.

Who can see it

Only you, when signed in. Each account's data is kept separate and the app refuses requests for records that belong to someone else. We don't sell, rent or share your data with third parties. It is stored on our hosting provider's servers, and emails are delivered through an email provider — both only process data to run the service. We may disclose data if required by law.

Security

Connections are encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, forms are protected against cross-site request forgery, and the database is backed up daily. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), your data is removed immediately from the live database; copies in rolling daily backups are overwritten within about two weeks. Demo accounts are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

Your choices and rights

  • Access & portability: download everything as CSV or JSON from Settings → Your data.
  • Correction: edit any entry, your name or your email at any time.
  • Deletion: delete your account and all its data from Settings.

Cookies

One essential cookie keeps you signed in and protects forms. If you're not signed in, your light/dark choice is saved in your browser's local storage. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

Children

Spendly isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and let signed-in users know in the app.
